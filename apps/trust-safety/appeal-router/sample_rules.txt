# The rulebook the original decisions cite. One rule per line.
R1: No unsolicited advertising, affiliate links, or promoting a product you profit from.
R2: Keep posts on the topic of the thread you are replying in.
R3: Criticise the argument, not the member. No insults or contempt aimed at a person.
R4: Do not post anyone's personal details, including your own.
R5: Claims about pricing or performance need a source link.
R6: No repeating the same post across multiple threads.
R7: No selling or trading between members outside the marketplace section.
