MERIDIAN CIVIC SYSTEMS LTD — RESPONSE TO TENDER REF. NCC/2026/0148
Citizen Casework Platform — Technical and Commercial Response
(Synthetic document, for demonstration only.)

1. INTRODUCTION

Meridian Civic Systems has delivered casework platforms to eleven UK local
authorities since 2017. This response sets out how Meridian Casework Cloud
meets the Authority's requirements.

2. HOSTING AND DATA RESIDENCY

Meridian Casework Cloud runs exclusively in AWS eu-west-2 (London). All
production data, all database replicas and all backup snapshots remain within
the United Kingdom for the full retention period. We warrant that no Authority
data, and no derivative of it, will be stored, replicated or processed outside
the United Kingdom during the term of the contract. Our sub-processor register
is appended at Annex C and every entry is UK-resident.

3. INFORMATION SECURITY CERTIFICATION

Meridian holds ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification (certificate no. UK-27001-4471,
issued by BSI) covering the development, hosting and support of the Meridian
Casework Cloud service. The certificate is valid to March 2028 and we commit to
maintaining certification, including surveillance audits, for the full term of
any contract awarded under this tender. A copy is attached at Annex A.

4. ACCESSIBILITY

All citizen-facing screens are built against WCAG 2.2 Level AA. We commit to
commissioning an independent accessibility audit by a third-party specialist
(our current partner is Dig Inclusion) and to supplying the Authority with the
full audit report and remediation log before go-live. Any Level A or AA issue
raised will be remediated before the service is released to the public.

5. SERVICE LEVELS

Meridian commits to a monthly service availability of 99.95%, measured at the
public endpoint and excluding agreed maintenance windows. Where measured
availability falls below 99.9% in any month, service credits are payable
against that month's charges on the sliding scale set out in Annex D. The SLA
and the credit regime are offered as contractual terms, not as targets.

6. INCIDENT AND BREACH NOTIFICATION

Our incident response procedure requires the Data Protection Officer to notify
the Authority's nominated contact within 24 hours of Meridian becoming aware of
any personal data breach affecting Authority data, followed by a written
incident report within five working days. This notification obligation is
offered as a binding contractual commitment.

7. SERVICE MANAGEMENT

A named Service Delivery Manager will be assigned to the Authority at contract
signature and will chair the monthly service review. The escalation path runs
SDM → Head of Service Operations → Chief Operating Officer, with contact
details maintained in the service handbook. Our contractual response target for
P1 incidents is 15 minutes to acknowledge and 4 hours to restore service or
provide a workaround.

8. EXIT AND DATA PORTABILITY

On expiry or termination, and at no additional charge, Meridian will export the
complete Authority dataset — cases, correspondence, attachments and audit
history — as newline-delimited JSON with a published schema, together with
attachments in their original formats. Exit assistance is included in the
recurring charge and is not a chargeable professional-services item.

9. INTEGRATION AND NOTIFICATIONS

The platform supports outbound email and SMS through a pluggable notification
layer, and we have integrated with a range of notification providers on
previous engagements. Integration with the Authority's preferred channel would
be scoped during mobilisation.

Meridian publishes a documented REST API. The Authority may extract case data
on demand through it; the OpenAPI description is published at
developers.meridiancivic.co.uk and access is included in the licence at no
extra cost.

10. TRAINING

Meridian will deliver role-based training for up to 250 Authority staff, split
by role (caseworker, supervisor, administrator, reporting). All sessions are
recorded and the recordings are hosted in the Authority's tenant of our
learning portal for the duration of the contract.

11. SECURITY TESTING

An independent CREST-accredited penetration test is carried out annually
against the production environment. Meridian commits to sharing the full report
and the associated remediation plan with the Authority within 20 working days
of test completion, and to remediating critical findings within 30 days.

12. PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT

Meridian invests continuously in the product and customers are kept informed of
significant developments through our customer community.

13. COMMERCIAL

Pricing is set out in the separate commercial schedule. Meridian confirms
acceptance of the Authority's standard terms and conditions subject to the
clarifications listed at Annex E.
