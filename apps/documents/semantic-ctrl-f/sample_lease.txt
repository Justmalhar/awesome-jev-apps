RESIDENTIAL TENANCY AGREEMENT — SAMPLE (synthetic, for demonstration only)

1. PARTIES. This Agreement is made between Northgate Property Holdings ("Landlord") and the undersigned ("Tenant") for the premises at Flat 4B, 118 Harbour Lane.

2. TERM. The tenancy runs for twelve (12) months commencing on the first day of the month following execution, and thereafter continues month to month until terminated under clause 14.

3. RENT. Rent is payable monthly in advance on the first day of each month. Payment received after the fifth day of the month is late.

4. LATE PAYMENT. A late fee of 4% of the outstanding monthly rent is charged on any rent not received by the fifth day of the month. A second late payment within any rolling twelve-month period increases this fee to 8%.

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a deposit equal to six weeks' rent. The deposit is held in a government-approved protection scheme and returned within 21 days of the end of the tenancy, less deductions itemised in writing.

6. UTILITIES. Tenant is responsible for electricity, gas, water, and internet. Landlord remains responsible for building insurance and communal area lighting.

7. PETS. No cat, dog, or other companion animal may be kept at the premises without the Landlord's prior written consent. Consent will not be unreasonably withheld for a single cat or a dog under 15kg. Assistance animals are permitted without consent and without additional charge or deposit.

8. SMOKING. Smoking and vaping are prohibited inside the premises and on balconies. Breach of this clause is grounds for termination under clause 14.

9. ALTERATIONS. Tenant shall not paint, drill, or otherwise alter the premises without written consent. Picture hooks and removable adhesive fixings do not require consent.

10. SUBLETTING. Tenant may not sublet the premises or any part of it. Tenant may host short-term guests for periods not exceeding fourteen (14) consecutive nights without notice to the Landlord.

11. ACCESS. Landlord may enter the premises for inspection or repair on giving at least 24 hours' written notice, except in an emergency, where no notice is required.

12. REPAIRS. Landlord repairs the structure, exterior, heating, and plumbing. Tenant is responsible for replacing light bulbs, smoke alarm batteries, and any damage caused by Tenant or Tenant's guests.

13. QUIET ENJOYMENT. Tenant shall not cause nuisance to neighbours. Noise audible outside the premises between 11pm and 7am constitutes a breach.

14. TERMINATION. Either party may terminate the periodic tenancy by giving two months' written notice. Landlord may terminate immediately for non-payment exceeding 21 days, for prohibited smoking, or for unauthorised subletting.

15. RENEWAL AND RENT REVIEW. Rent may be reviewed once per twelve-month period. Any increase requires two months' written notice and may not exceed 6% in a single review.

16. INSURANCE. Tenant is strongly advised to obtain contents insurance. Landlord's policy does not cover Tenant's possessions under any circumstances.

17. END OF TENANCY. Tenant shall return the premises professionally cleaned, with all keys returned. Carpets must be professionally cleaned where pets have been kept under clause 7.

18. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement is governed by the law of the jurisdiction in which the premises are situated.
