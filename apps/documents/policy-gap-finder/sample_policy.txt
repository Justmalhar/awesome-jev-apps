NORTHWIND ANALYTICS LTD — INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
Version 4.2 · Owner: Chief Information Security Officer · Classification: Internal
(Synthetic document, for demonstration only.)

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This policy sets out how Northwind protects the confidentiality, integrity and
availability of the information it holds, including customer data processed in
the Northwind platform. It applies to all employees, contractors and interns,
and to all systems operated by or on behalf of Northwind.

2. GOVERNANCE AND REVIEW

This policy is approved by the Executive Committee. The CISO is accountable for
maintaining it and presents it to the Executive Committee for re-approval every
twelve months, and additionally within one month of any material change to the
platform architecture, the regulatory environment or the corporate structure.
The approved version is published on the intranet policy register and all staff
are notified by email when a new version is issued.

3. SECURITY ROLES

Security responsibilities are allocated as follows and recorded in the security
RACI held by the CISO:

  - Chief Information Security Officer — owns this policy, the risk register
    and the annual security programme.
  - Head of Platform Engineering — owns technical controls in production,
    including access control, encryption and patching.
  - Data Protection Officer — owns the record of processing, data subject
    rights and breach notification to regulators.
  - People Operations Manager — owns joiner, mover and leaver processes.
  - Every line manager — owns access approvals for their direct reports.

4. ACCESS CONTROL

4.1 All access to Northwind systems is granted on the principle of least
privilege and requires documented approval from the requester's line manager
before it is provisioned.

4.2 Privileged access to production — root, database superuser, cloud
administrator and CI deployment roles — is restricted to named members of the
Platform Engineering on-call rota. Standing privileged access is not permitted;
privileges are obtained through the just-in-time elevation workflow, expire
after four hours and are logged. The Head of Platform Engineering reviews the
full list of privileged role holders on the first working day of each quarter
and revokes anything no longer justified. The review record is retained for
three years.

4.3 Authentication to all corporate and production systems is through the
company identity provider using single sign-on. Phishing-resistant multi-factor
authentication (FIDO2 hardware keys or platform passkeys) is mandatory for all
staff and is enforced technically by conditional access policy; SMS and
time-based one-time codes are not accepted for production access. Service
accounts use short-lived workload identity credentials rather than static
secrets, and the Head of Platform Engineering is responsible for enforcement.

5. PEOPLE SECURITY

5.1 Pre-employment screening. People Operations obtains and verifies identity,
right to work, two employment references and a basic criminal record check for
every candidate before their start date. Candidates who will hold privileged
production access or handle customer data additionally undergo a financial
probity check. No candidate is granted system access until screening has
completed, and People Operations records the outcome in the HR system.

5.2 Security awareness. All staff complete security awareness training within
their first week and a refresher module annually thereafter. Engineers complete
an additional secure development module annually. Simulated phishing exercises
are run quarterly by the security team, and staff who fail two consecutive
exercises are enrolled in targeted coaching by their line manager. People
Operations tracks completion and reports it to the Executive Committee each
quarter.

5.3 Leavers. Confidentiality obligations and the return of company information
continue to apply after employment ends. Departing staff are reminded of these
obligations. Access is withdrawn when someone leaves.

6. OPERATIONS

6.1 Incident management. Suspected security incidents are reported to
security@northwind.example or through the #security-incident channel, which is
monitored 24/7 by the on-call rota. The CISO is the incident manager for any
incident rated P1 or P2. Triage begins within 30 minutes of a P1 report; the
Data Protection Officer is engaged within one hour where personal data may be
affected, and regulator notification is assessed against the 72-hour statutory
deadline. Every P1 incident receives a written post-incident review within ten
working days, owned by the CISO.

6.2 Backups. Production databases and object storage are backed up nightly,
encrypted at rest with customer-managed keys, and retained for 35 days with a
monthly snapshot retained for one year. Backups are replicated to a second
region. Platform Engineering performs a full restore rehearsal into an isolated
environment every quarter and records the measured restore time; a failed
rehearsal is raised as a P2 incident.

6.3 Logging and monitoring. Application, infrastructure and identity provider
logs are shipped to the central log platform and retained for 400 days. Log
coverage is kept broad so that investigations have the data they need.

7. SUPPLIERS

Northwind recognises that suppliers and sub-processors can introduce risk to
customer data, and is committed to working only with suppliers that take
security seriously. Security considerations form part of procurement decisions.

8. BUSINESS CONTINUITY

Northwind maintains a business continuity plan covering the loss of the primary
cloud region and the loss of the main office. The plan is held by the Chief
Operating Officer. The company intends to exercise the plan periodically.

9. COMPLIANCE

Breaches of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including
dismissal, and for contractors, termination of engagement.
